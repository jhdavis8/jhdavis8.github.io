I am a second-year computer science Ph.D. student at the [University of Maryland](https://www.cs.umd.edu/) in College Park, MD, where I am advised by [Prof. Abhinav Bhatele](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~bhatele/). 

As a member of the [Parallel Software and Systems Group](https://pssg.cs.umd.edu/), the primary subject of my research as a doctoral student is performance portability, or the ability of a single-source application to achieve good performance on a range of hardware platforms.

In my time at UMD I have also collaborated with [the CRoCCo Lab](http://croccolab.umd.edu/) on adding GPU acceleration and adaptive mesh refinement to their finite-difference computational fluid dynamics code [using AMReX](https://amrex-codes.github.io/amrex/). We will be presenting the results of this work along with extensive performance analysis at [IPDPS 2023](https://www.ipdps.org/ipdps2023/2023-advance-program.html).

I graduated from the [University of Delaware](https://www.cis.udel.edu/) with a B.S. in Computer Science as well as a B.A. in Philosophy and minor in Mathematics in May 2021. At UD, I worked with the [Computational Research and Programming Lab](https://crpl.cis.udel.edu/), led by Prof. Sunita Chandrasekaran, as a developer for the [SOLLVE OpenMP V&V test suite](https://github.com/SOLLVE/sollve_vv), part of the Exascale Computing Project (ECP). To see the latest results from the V&V suite, check out [their website](https://crpl.cis.udel.edu/ompvvsollve/results/).

At the moment I am additionally involved with automatically formally verifying data structures from [<i>The Art of Multiprocessor Programming</i>](https://www.elsevier.com/books/the-art-of-multiprocessor-programming/herlihy/978-0-12-415950-1), using [CIVL](http://vsl.cis.udel.edu/civl/), collaborating with the [Verified Software Lab](http://vsl.cis.udel.edu/) at UD, led by Prof. Stephen Siegel. More on that project can be found [here on GitHub](https://github.com/jhdavis8/amp-ver).
